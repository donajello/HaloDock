# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- **Any edge but the bottom.** The wheel can now open from the left, right and/or top edge. Pick them on the new Screen edges page.
- **Ring ripple.** Switching rings sends a wave of the new ring's colour out from the edge.
- **Ring dots by the edge.** The coloured ring dots now sit in a neat column right against the screen edge.
- **New Settings window.** Rebuilt from scratch: a fast, lightweight dark/light design with a sidebar (Rings, Screen edges, Behavior, Updates). Changes apply instantly. Drag rows to reorder, drop files in, and unpin with one click. The version number is shown in the title bar and sidebar.
- **Optional updates.** Off by default. When on, HaloDock checks GitHub once a day and can download and install a new release for you. "Check for updates" is also in the tray menu.
- **Rings open full.** A ring you haven't scrolled yet opens on its middle item rather than its first.
- **Fix:** "Add items…" showed as garbled text ("Add itemsâ€¦").
- **Fix:** edits made in the wheel while Settings is open no longer get overwritten by it.

## 0.3.0

- **Unpin to remove.** While you hold an icon, an unpin symbol appears in the middle of the wheel. Drop the icon on it to take it off the ring (the file itself is never touched).
- **Pin to add.** Files dragged in from Explorer show a pin in the gap where they'll be added.
- **Choose your symbols.** Settings → Behavior offers matching remove/add pairs: unpin/pin (default), minus/plus, eye-slash/eye, star-slash/star, cross/plus. The right-click menu uses the same wording.
- **Fix:** on a small wheel, files dragged between two folders always went into one of them. Gaps between icons are reachable again.

## 0.2.0

- **Renamed** from EdgeDock to HaloDock. Settings and the start-with-Windows entry carry over automatically.
- **Arrange like a phone home screen.** Long-press an icon (or press and drag) to pick it up; the others part to show where it'll land. Let go off the ring to cancel.
- **Drag to add.** Drop files from Explorer into a gap between icons to add them to the ring.
- **Remove from ring** in the right-click menu.

## 0.1.0

- First version: a half-wheel of files, folders and apps that opens from the left edge of the screen.
- Real Windows icons, scroll to turn, click to open, the normal right-click menu.
- Drag files onto a folder to move/copy them, or onto an app to open them with it.
- Multiple rings, switched with coloured dots on an inner ring.
- Multi-monitor options, and light/dark theme to match Windows.
